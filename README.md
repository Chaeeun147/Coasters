# Coasters
Pokémon Themed Coasters!!

This is my 3D modelled custom coaster for cups!

I made this because I really like woodworking, and for some reason, one of my favourite things I made was coasters. I would make blank coasters and laser-print a variety of pictures on each one. I would make custom coasters and give them to my friends, family, etc. So, when I learned CAD, I thought, "I can continue my love for coasters and make them with 3D printers!!" Therefore, I decided to make this project!
